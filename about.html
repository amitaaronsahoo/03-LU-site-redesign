Meet Fran

Francine "Fran" Miller has always had a green thumb. Growing up in the Bay Area, she spent her weekends helping her grandmother in the garden. In 1995, she decided to turn her passion into a business.

When she isn't arranging beautiful bouquets, Fran enjoys volunteering at the local community garden and spending time with her two golden retrievers.
